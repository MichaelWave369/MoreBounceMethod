# The More Bounce Method™

**Become more yourself. Harm less. Give more. Leave more life behind.**

An open, evolving practice for becoming more honest, compassionate, creative, accountable, and connected, while making life a little better for other people and the world around us.

## Six B.O.U.N.C.E. principles

- **B: Be Real** — Practice honesty, especially with yourself.
- **O: Own Your Impact** — Take responsibility and repair harm.
- **U: Understand Before Judging** — Listen with curiosity and humility.
- **N: Nurture Interconnection** — Care for people, animals, communities, and ecosystems.
- **C: Create More Than You Destroy** — Contribute more good than harm.
- **E: Evolve With Evidence** — Learn, update, and welcome correction.

## Fundamental safeguards

There is no score of human worth, no obligation to be happy, no competition for moral perfection, and no requirement to share private reflections. Rest, healthy boundaries, and asking for help are valid ways to practice More Bounce.

**Ledger Above Ego.** This is a beginning, not a claim to have solved human nature.
