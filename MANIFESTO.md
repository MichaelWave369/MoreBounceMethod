# The More Bounce Manifesto

**Become more yourself. Harm less. Give more. Leave more life behind.**

We reject the idea that a person's worth can be measured by wealth, productivity, popularity, or constant happiness.

We believe honesty without cruelty, compassion without self-erasure, curiosity without arrogance, and responsibility without shame are skills worth practicing.

We don't need more copies of one person. We need space for everyone to be their own deeply individual, imperfect, compassionate selves without stepping on others.

## B.O.U.N.C.E.

- **Be Real.** Let truth matter more than appearances. Say "I don't know."
- **Own Your Impact.** Good intentions are not substitutes for care. Repair when you can.
- **Understand Before Judging.** Listen well without abandoning boundaries or critical thought.
- **Nurture Interconnection.** Everything we do affects living beings and places around us.
- **Create More Than You Destroy.** Give something useful, kind, beautiful, or sustaining to the world.
- **Evolve With Evidence.** Change your mind when a better understanding arrives. Ledger above ego.

## Rules we will not compromise

1. No virtue points, moral leaderboard, or ranking of people.
2. No forced happiness or pressure to give what you cannot safely give.
3. No shame for disability, grief, hardship, or days when surviving is enough.
4. No demand that a person reveal private information or experiences.
5. No religion, ideology, or worldview required to participate.
6. No claim that this is a clinically proven program or universal cure.
7. No unchallengeable founder, guru, or gatekeeper: questions and improvements belong here.

"The measure of a life is not how much of the world you acquire, but how much better the world becomes because you were here."

**Nobody owns the Bounce. Everybody can add to it.**

More Bounce. Less Fear. Ledger Above Ego.
